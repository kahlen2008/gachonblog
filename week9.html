dorm cooking

after weeks of eating outside, grabbing cafeteria meals, and relying on quick convenience store bites, we were desperately craving a wholesome, home-cooked meal. we made a grocery trip down to the nearby e-mart just outside our school and loaded our baskets with fresh ingredients: thinly sliced beef, crisp chye sim, enoki mushrooms, and hearty blocks of tofu. back in our dorm kitchen, we cooked everything together into a steaming, fragrant soup. there was something so deeply comforting about watching the leafy greens wilt into the bubbling broth alongside the tender beef. sitting in our room eating a warm, nourishing bowl packed with veggies and clean flavors felt like an absolute hug from home and gave us the perfect nutritional boost to tackle our exam preparation.
