overview & the gachon gift

week 3 has been all about settling into our routine and digging deeper into seoul! oh, and a funny story—we actually received a welcome gift from gachon university back in week 2, but we completely forgot to open it and see what they gave us until now! it was such a nice surprise discovering all the cute university merch hidden inside. they gave us some adorable items that i'm definitely going to use for my classes. it really made us feel so welcomed and officially part of the student body!

gachon merch!

myeongdong revisited

myeongdong kyoja 🥟

we decided to revisit myeongdong this week with a very specific mission: myeongdong kyoja! it's the most famous dumpling and noodle restaurant in the district and has been operating since 1966. last week, the staff told us the waiting time was an hour, so we reluctantly had to miss out. but this time we were absolutely determined! even though the queue was still quite long when we arrived, they move incredibly fast and we managed to get a table within just 15 minutes! the food was so worth it—the rich, garlicky kalguksu (knife-cut noodles) and the plump, juicy mandu (dumplings) were phenomenal. it's easy to see why this place has maintained its legendary status for nearly 60 years!

after that satisfying meal, we couldn't resist diving back into the vibrant street food scene. the atmosphere was electric, with neon lights everywhere. we bought some freshly grilled cheese on a stick for 5000 won, and it tasted unbelievably good.

grilled cheese!
the coin! 🪙

but the absolute highlight was finding the famous cheese and custard coin! it was also only 5000 won, and honestly, i have been waiting to revisit this exact treat since 2024. biting into that warm, sweet, oozing coin brought back so many amazing memories. it was the perfect dessert to end our myeongdong adventure!

campus life & cafe

we officially got enrolled into all our courses this week! this is what gachon university's version of politemall looks like—they call it cyber campus. it's where all our course materials, syllabuses, and important announcements get uploaded. navigating a completely new learning management system was a bit confusing at first, but it feels so official now that we can see all our modules listed out!

cyber campus!
a twosome place 🍰

between lectures, we've been hunting for the best study spots on campus. "a twosome place" is a popular coffeeshop located right in our school, and we finally gave it a try. the chocolate cake there tasted really good and was the perfect sugar rush to get us through the rest of the day. we spent a couple of hours just sipping our drinks, reviewing our syllabuses, and people-watching as students rushed between buildings.

speaking of lectures, classes are officially in full swing! i attended my operating systems lecture this week. it’s definitely going to be a challenging module, but learning complex technical topics in a completely new environment makes the rigorous coursework feel a bit more exciting! the professor seems really passionate, and despite the whole new setting, i think it's going to be a really rewarding class.

os lecture 💻

dormitory meals

dorm food!

for dinner, the 3rd dormitory meal hall has become our ultimate go-to spot. it is extremely convenient for us since we stay at the 1st dormitory, which is just a stone's throw away. honestly, it's so much better than walking all the way down those infamously steep campus hills to vision tower for dinner, especially because there are no more ladybug shuttle buses running at that time. after a long, exhausting day of classes, having a hot meal so close to home is a massive blessing. fun fact: eating on campus is incredibly affordable! breakfast is only 1000 won (less than $1!), while lunch and dinner are only 6000 won for the places that sell food in gachon. the portions are always generous, and it really makes budgeting for our exchange semester so much easier.

dongdaemun trip

over the weekend, we took a trip to dongdaemun, a vibrant district where history seamlessly blends with the future. we started by admiring the magnificent heunginjimun (dongdaemun gate). standing proudly amidst the bustling modern traffic and towering high-rises, this centuries-old fortress gate is a breathtaking reminder of seoul's rich past. it was surreal to see cars zooming past a structure that has guarded the city for hundreds of years. we spent a good amount of time just walking around the perimeter and soaking in the contrast between the old and the new.

historic gate
ddp!

just a short walk away from the historic gate is the truly stunning dongdaemun design plaza (ddp). the transition from traditional architecture to this futuristic, spaceship-like landmark gave us total whiplash in the best way possible. the smooth, metallic curves of the building were absolutely mesmerizing to walk through, especially as the evening approached and the building began to brilliantly light up. we spent ages exploring the unique art exhibitions inside and taking tons of aesthetic photos along the staircases.

after all that sightseeing, we stumbled upon a massive stationery market nearby, and we completely lost track of time. we spent almost two full hours just browsing through the endless aisles. from incredibly cute planners to aesthetic highlighters and unique stickers, it was basically heaven. hauling bags of adorable school supplies that we definitely didn't need but absolutely couldn't resist, we felt so accomplished. my desk back in the dorm is going to be fully decorated now. it's amazing how shopping for school supplies can feel like such a massive, joyous event here in korea!

stationery haul ✏️

what we learned

  • ✔ sometimes the longest queues really do mean the best food
  • ✔ on-campus meals are the ultimate life hack for saving money
  • ✔ never underestimate how much stationery you can buy in one sitting
  • ✔ exploring historical and futuristic sites in one day is the magic of seoul

another amazing week in the books! seoul just keeps surprising us, and i'm so grateful for every chaotic, delicious, and unforgettable moment... ✈️🤍
