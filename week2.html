overview

week 2 was all about finally settling into a routine and really starting to feel like locals! classes officially started, which meant getting lost while exploring our absolutely huge campus. beyond the university grounds, we ventured out to discover some of the most delicious local food in suseo, enjoyed a relaxing and picturesque trip to seokchon lake with our entire orientation group, and spent hours doing some much-needed window shopping and hauling in the vibrant streets of myeongdong and the trendy alleyways of seongsu. every single day felt like a new adventure waiting to unfold!

first classes & campus life

database lecture 💻

the first week of actual classes was definitely a completely new experience! our campus is incredibly hilly, so rushing to get to class on time truly felt like a full-blown leg day workout every single morning. my database lecture and discrete math classes turned out to be super interesting, although i’m definitely still getting used to the faster pace and the different teaching styles the professors use here. stepping into the lecture halls for the first time was awe-inspiring—they are absolutely massive, exceptionally well-equipped, and feel so modern compared to what i'm used to back home!

between our lectures, we spent a lot of time wandering around and exploring the iconic campus landmarks. gachon university is incredibly famous for its unique and striking architecture, especially the massive infinity symbol structure that anchors the center of the school. but another legendary icon is the 'ladybug'—gachon's beloved, brightly colored shuttle bus! it conveniently operates every 10 minutes on weekdays (though we quickly learned that sometimes it's even faster). unfortunately, it completely stops running when it rains or snows for safety reasons, but on clear, sunny days, it is an absolute lifesaver. it gets us effortlessly up and down the incredibly steep hills, saving our tired legs and heavily cutting down our morning commute time. we honestly couldn't resist taking so many pictures around the campus! the panoramic views from the topmost stop where the ladybug drops us off are absolutely breathtaking, offering a sweeping look at the surrounding lush hills and the sprawling city below.

what a view!
campus friends ♡

hanging out by the grand infinity symbol quickly became one of our orientation group's absolute favorite meeting spots. there's always a lively crowd there! spontaneously running into friends between lectures, grabbing a quick iced americano from a nearby cafe, and eagerly comparing our busy schedules made the massive, overwhelming campus feel so much more intimate and connected. the university genuinely feels like its own vibrant little self-sustaining town, and having these beautiful central spots to sit back and catch up with familiar faces easily became the highlight of our daily routine.

suseo & campus eats

kbbq night!

we quickly discovered that suseo, just a short and convenient subway ride away from our dorm, is an absolute treasure trove for amazing, affordable food! we knew we absolutely had to try authentic korean bbq during our first week, so we treated ourselves to some incredible so-galbisal. it's a highly popular korean bbq (gogi-gui) cut that is incredibly well-known for its beautifully marbled, juicy texture and its remarkably rich, beefy flavour profile. watching the thick, seasoned cuts of beef sizzle and caramelize on the hot charcoal grill was completely mouth-watering. more importantly, observing the locals and learning how to carefully construct the perfect, flavor-packed lettuce wrap with garlic and ssamjang was an unforgettable cultural and culinary experience!

another day, while blindly wandering the streets, we stumbled upon this incredibly cozy little beef bowl store tucked away in suseo. the entire menu was written exclusively in korean, which was definitely a bit of a daunting challenge at first (though it was nothing the trusty papago translation app couldn't fix!). taking a bit of a gamble, we ended up ordering their signature gyudon, and it was simply out of this world! the thinly sliced beef was incredibly tender and steeped in a perfectly sweet and savory broth. it was so incredibly hearty and comforting, providing exactly the kind of warm, soul-soothing meal we desperately needed after a long, exhausting day of back-to-back lectures and endlessly hiking up and down the steep campus hills.

delicious gyudon 🍚
ramen fix 🍜

the best part is that we didn't even have to travel far for good food! right outside our school's main gate, just before you hit the gachon university subway station entrance, there is an entire bustling stretch of amazing eateries and tiny restaurants. craving something soupy, we stopped by a popular local spot for some hot, comforting ramen one chilly afternoon, and the rich, complex broth totally hit the spot. realizing that we have such a massive variety of delicious and budget-friendly food options literally right on our doorstep makes living on campus so incredibly convenient and exciting.

seokchon lake trip with the orientation group

seokchon lake 🦆

to celebrate the end of our first full week, our orientation group planned a fun weekend trip to the famous seokchon lake! we got incredibly lucky because the crisp autumn weather was absolutely perfect for a long, leisurely stroll. walking around the expansive, glittering lake with the towering, futuristic silhouette of the lotte world tower looming majestically in the background felt completely magical. we ended up spending hours simply strolling along the paved paths, chatting about our shared culture shocks, taking endless group pictures, and really taking the time to get to know each other better in a relaxed setting far outside of the busy campus environment.

after our extensive walk around the lake, we were completely starving, so we navigated the nearby streets to grab dinner together at a bustling local restaurant. we went all out and ordered a massive, mouth-watering spread featuring bossam (tender, slow-boiled pork belly meant for wrapping) and a giant gamja-chae-jeon (a perfectly crispy, shredded potato pancake). it was actually my very first time trying these specific traditional dishes, and the incredible contrast of flavors and textures instantly made them some of my all-time favorites! laughing, chatting, and sharing such a huge, communal meal with everyone from different corners of the world really made our new friend group start to feel like a tight-knit family.

the best feast!
the whole crew 💖

after navigating such a long, overwhelming, and physically tiring first week of adjusting to a new country, ending it surrounded by this wonderful group of people was exactly the comforting reset we needed. we made sure to snap a chaotic but happy final group photo before finally parting ways for the night and heading back to the dorms. it's so fascinating how everyone brings such wildly different perspectives and stories from their respective home countries, yet we all share the exact same boundless excitement and nervous energy for the entire semester ahead. i'm already eagerly looking forward to planning many more weekend trips and creating unforgettable memories with them!

city shopping

of course, no first week in korea would be complete without hitting up some of seoul's most famous and bustling shopping districts! we took the subway all the way to myeongdong and specifically went out of our way to visit the massive, beautifully designed apple store located right in the heart of the district. the architecture and layout were absolutely stunning! but even more impressive was the sheer energy of myeongdong itself—it is so incredibly vibrant and alive, with countless aromatic street food vendors lining the center of the main roads and endless, brightly lit stores calling your name from every single direction.

myeongdong vibes!
shopping spree!

while excitedly walking through the densely packed, bustling streets, we simply couldn't resist stopping every few minutes to try some of the incredibly famous local street food. the variety was staggering—there was absolutely everything you could imagine, from piping hot, freshly fried hotteok (sweet syrup-filled pancakes) to savory grilled meat skewers and crunchy, sugar-coated tanghulu! as the sun went down, the brilliant, colorful neon signs lighting up the narrow, labyrinth-like alleys created the absolute perfect cinematic backdrop for us as we spent the evening popping in and out of the endless array of cosmetic stores, flagship brand shops, and quirky cute boutiques.

later in the week, we also made a point to explore seongsu-dong, a rapidly gentrifying neighborhood that is often fondly referred to as the brooklyn of seoul. it’s an incredibly cool area, completely filled to the brim with highly aesthetic, industrial-chic cafes, trendy pop-up shops, and unique vintage clothing stores. naturally, for any exchange student, a dedicated trip to olive young was absolutely mandatory. we got exceptionally lucky because we happened to visit right when they were hosting their massive, highly anticipated 'big sale' event! getting the chance to stock up on all the latest, wildly trending korean skincare serums and makeup products at such an unbelievable discount was an incredible feeling. our wallets definitely felt a little bit lighter by the time we hauled our heavy shopping bags back to the dorms, but honestly, experiencing the thrill of korean beauty shopping firsthand made it completely worth it!

skincare heaven ✨

what we learned

  • ✔ gachon's hills are the ultimate leg day workout
  • ✔ never trust a menu without pictures, but also sometimes take the risk!
  • ✔ seoul's public transport is incredibly efficient and easy to navigate
  • ✔ korean skincare really lives up to the hype

week 2 was packed with learning, exploring, and eating! we can't wait to see what week 3 has in store... ♡
