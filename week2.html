overview

week 2 was all about finally settling into a routine! classes officially started, which meant exploring our huge campus! we also explored beyond the university, checking out delicious food in suseo, relaxing at seokchon lake with our orientation group, and doing some much-needed shopping in myeongdong and seongsu.

first classes & campus life

database lecture 💻

the first week of classes was definitely an experience! our campus is incredibly hilly, so getting to class felt like a workout every single morning. my database lecture and discrete math classes were super interesting, though i’m still getting used to the different teaching styles here. the lecture halls are huge and feel so modern!

between classes, we spent a lot of time exploring the campus landmarks. gachon university is famous for its iconic architecture, like the massive infinity symbol structure. but another icon is the 'ladybug'—gachon's shuttle bus! it operates every 10 minutes on weekdays (though sometimes it's a little faster). unfortunately, it doesn't run when it rains or snows, but on clear days, it gets us up and down the incredibly steep hills, saving our legs and heavily cutting down our commute time. we took so many pictures around the campus! the views from where the ladybug drops us off are also absolutely breathtaking, overlooking the surrounding hills and city.

what a view!
campus friends ♡

hanging out by the infinity symbol became one of our orientation groups' favorite meeting spots. running into friends between lectures, grabbing a quick coffee, and comparing our schedules made the massive campus feel so much more connected. the university really feels like its own vibrant little town, and having these central spots to catch up with familiar faces is the best part of the day.

suseo & campus eats

kbbq night!

we discovered that suseo, just a short subway ride away, is a treasure trove for amazing food! we had to try authentic korean bbq, so we treated ourselves to some incredible samgyeopsal. watching the thick cuts of pork belly sizzle on the grill was mouth-watering, and learning how to make the perfect lettuce wrap from the locals was incredible.

another day, we found this cozy little beef store in suseo. the menu was entirely in korean, which was a bit of a challenge (not anything papago can't fix though!), but the gyudon we ended up ordering was out of this world! it was so hearty and comforting, exactly what we needed after a long day of lectures and walking up and down the campus hills.

delicious gyudon 🍚
ramen fix 🍜

we didn't even have to go far for good food! right outside our school, just before the gachon university subway station, there's a whole stretch of amazing eateries. we stopped by for some hot, comforting ramen one afternoon, and it totally hit the spot. having so many food options literally on our doorstep is amazing.

seokchon lake trip with the orientation group

seokchon lake 🦆

over the weekend, our orientation group planned a trip to seokchon lake! the weather was absolutely perfect for a stroll. walking around the lake with the view of lotte world tower in the background was magical. we spent hours just chatting, taking pictures, and getting to know each other better outside of the campus environment.

after our walk, we were starving, so we grabbed dinner together. we ordered a massive spread of bossam (boiled pork wrap) and gamja-chae-jeon (potato pancake). it was my first time trying these dishes and they instantly became some of my favorites! sharing a huge meal with everyone really made us feel like a tight-knit family.

the best feast!
the whole crew 💖

after such a long and tiring week, ending it with this group was exactly what we needed. we took a final group photo before parting ways for the night. everyone brings such different perspectives from their home countries, but we all share the same excitement for the semester ahead. i'm really looking forward to more weekend trips with them!

city shopping

of course, we had to hit up some of seoul's most famous shopping districts! we went to myeongdong and visited the massive apple store there. it was stunning! the energy in myeongdong is so vibrant, with street food vendors lining the roads and endless stores calling your name.

myeongdong vibes!
shopping spree!

walking through the bustling streets, we couldn't resist trying some of the famous street food. there was everything from hotteok (sweet pancakes) to skewers and tanghulu! the bright neon signs lighting up the narrow alleys created the perfect backdrop for popping into the endless cosmetic stores and cute boutiques.

we also explored seongsu-dong, often called the brooklyn of seoul. it’s filled with aesthetic cafes and trendy shops. naturally, a trip to olive young was mandatory, especially since they were having their massive big sale! stocking up on all the trending korean skincare and makeup products at such a discount was incredible. our wallets definitely felt a little lighter after that trip, but it was completely worth it!

skincare heaven ✨

what we learned

  • ✔ gachon's hills are the ultimate leg day workout
  • ✔ never trust a menu without pictures, but also sometimes take the risk!
  • ✔ seoul's public transport is incredibly efficient and easy to navigate
  • ✔ korean skincare really lives up to the hype

week 2 was packed with learning, exploring, and eating! we can't wait to see what week 3 has in store... ♡
