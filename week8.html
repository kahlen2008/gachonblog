academics & midterms

midterm season officially kicked into full gear this week, and the academic grind has been intense! back at nyp, we never actually defined or labeled tests as "midterms" or "final exams", so just hearing these two terms thrown around here feels pretty daunting and carries a whole new level of pressure. on top of that, in our database class, we spent hours practicing how to draw entity-relationship (er) diagrams using the university's notation. at nyp, we were completely accustomed to barker's notation, but here at gachon, we've had to master chen's notation—mapping out rectangular entities, diamond-shaped relationships, and oval attributes with precise 1:N cardinalities and double participation lines. to add to the challenge, midterms here are strictly written with pen and paper! this was such a foreign and unfamiliar experience for us, because back at nyp, every single test and assignment is conducted digitally on our laptops. drawing intricate diagrams by hand without the luxury of an undo button, backspace, or being able to click and drag boxes around meant we had to plan each layout carefully with pencils and rulers before committing with pen!

erd practice 📊
kahoot practice! 🏆

to help us prepare, our professor held another kahoot session in class, which was kinda like our last practice before our test to really test our speed and accuracy! on top of that, this gave bonus marks again, which definitely raised the stakes and made everyone extra locked in. it was such an exciting boost and a fun way to lock in some extra marks and revision right before exam day. when we had our midterms though, it wasn't really clear at first how much time we had, but we found out that it was 30 minutes in the end. it felt a little rushed in practice, especially considering that the students in the front row got their papers about five minutes before the people in the last row got theirs, and there wasn't extra time given. but it's totally okay because we studied really hard beforehand, so we got through the paper with not a lot of difficulty!

dorm cooking

after weeks of eating outside, grabbing cafeteria meals, and relying on quick convenience store bites, we were desperately craving a wholesome, home-cooked meal. we made a grocery trip down to the nearby e-mart just outside our school and loaded our baskets with fresh ingredients: thinly sliced beef, crisp chye sim, enoki mushrooms, and hearty blocks of tofu. back in our dorm kitchen, we cooked everything together into a steaming, fragrant soup. there was something so deeply comforting about watching the leafy greens wilt into the bubbling broth alongside the tender beef. sitting in our room eating a warm, nourishing bowl packed with veggies and clean flavors felt like an absolute hug from home and gave us the perfect nutritional boost to tackle our exam preparation.

dorm hotpot 🍲

campus quirks

the campus ladybug 🐞

i already talked about our campus shuttle bus back in week 2, but i finally managed to get a proper picture of how the famous "ladybug" shuttle train (무당이 / moodang-i) actually looks like from the outside! it's an open-air red tram adorned with black spots that shuttles students up and down the steep university hills. while we've ridden it countless times and shared views from the inside before, this week it was parked right outside the ai building under a clear blue autumn sky, so i finally snapped a full shot of the entire train. it genuinely looks like an adorable amusement park ride!

on the subject of campus quirks, our evening walks back to the dormitory have recently felt like walking through a spooky halloween movie set! autumn in korea brings out giant joro spiders, and on our walk up the hill, we spotted multiple colossal spiderwebs spun high between tree branches and campus light poles. one web in particular was truly enormous—suspended high in the air right against the backdrop of the dorm buildings, with a massive yellow-and-black spider sitting calmly in the center, surrounded by dozens of tiny trapped insects. while it's fascinating to observe korea's autumn wildlife up close, it definitely taught us to watch where we're walking at night so we don't end up walking face-first into a giant web!

giant spiderwebs! 🕸️

grad school food

grad school lunch 🍱

for lunch one afternoon, we decided to explore another dining option on campus and headed over to the graduate school of education cafeteria. we ordered their daily set, which featured a piping hot, savory and spicy pork soup with noodles and crunchy bean sprouts, accompanied by a juicy hamburg steak, creamy macaroni salad, and tender seasoned potatoes for 6,000 won. the graduate school of education food is slightly pricier compared to other student cafeterias around campus, with lunch set at 6,000 won and dinner at 8,000 won. the meal itself was warm, hearty, and deeply satisfying—especially on a brisk afternoon. but if you don't mind walking down the hill to eat, my personal opinion is still to head straight down to hi dojo! for roughly the same price, hi dojo's japanese katsu and donburi are simply unbeatable. still, no complaints at all about the grad school food—it hit the spot nicely between study sessions!

suseo & yoajung

later in the week, we took a quick trip down to suseo to unwind. as late afternoon rolled in, we were greeted by the most stunning autumn sunset over the city. the sky was illuminated in fiery orange, golden amber, and soft lavender hues, with dramatic glowing clouds stretching across the mountain ridge and apartment skyline. it was one of those peaceful moments that made us stop in our tracks, take a deep breath, and appreciate just how beautiful korea is in mid-october.

sunset at suseo 🌅
yoajung cafe 🍨

while in suseo, we made it our mission to visit yoajung (요아정), the viral korean yogurt dessert cafe! the outlet is tucked away in a quiet, cozy corner of suseo, away from the busy main road. the store's ambience was so pleasant and welcoming, featuring clean pastel blue-and-white decor and warm overhead lighting. one small detail we really appreciated was that they thoughtfully provide both dry tissues and wet wipes on the counter for customers—a super considerate touch that made our dining experience even more comfortable.

and now for the dessert itself—we ordered their signature frozen yogurt bowl with crisp chocolate shells, crunchy granola, and rich golden honey for 8,500 won. we actually planned to get the honeycomb as well, but unfortunately it was completely sold out! you'll have to stay tuned for next week to see whether we finally get to try it. nonetheless, the portion size was so generous that it can easily be shared between two people, making it incredible value for money. the taste was pure perfection: the tart, refreshing frozen yogurt paired with the rich, crackling chocolate shell and sweet honeyed granola created the most addictive crunch and flavor balance. honestly, i find it way better than llaollao and yolé back home in singapore! it was hands-down the sweetest reward after days of intense midterm revision.

sweet perfection! 🍯

f1 night race

singapore gp stream 🏎️

back in the dorm over the weekend, we tuned into the live stream of the singapore grand prix! it felt so surreal and nostalgic to watch the cars tear through the neon-lit marina bay street circuit under the singapore airlines banners while sitting over four thousand kilometers away in south korea. seeing our familiar home skyline illuminated in the middle of the night race gave us a sudden pang of home pride and nostalgia. it was the perfect cozy dorm evening, cheering on the drivers and feeling connected to home while living abroad.

  • ✔ pen-and-paper midterms require serious precision—no ctrl+z for er diagrams!
  • ✔ kahoot speed and accuracy practice earns bonus marks (and an all-nyp podium!)
  • ✔ dorm-cooked beef and chye sim hotpot is the best soul food away from home
  • ✔ finally got a proper picture of the iconic ladybug train outside the ai building
  • ✔ watch your head on the dorm walk because korean joro spiders build mansions
  • ✔ yoajung easily beats llaollao and yolé (even with honeycomb sold out—stay tuned for next week!)
  • ✔ streaming the singapore grand prix from korea brings the warmest touch of home

from pen-and-paper exams to refreshing yoajung — week 8 is in the books! 📝🍦
