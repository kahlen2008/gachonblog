academics & classes

back to academics, we've started diving into the hands-on portion of our database class! we are currently learning how to write sql queries. to make sure we truly understand the concepts, our professor created an incredibly helpful html notebook for us. it's completely interactive and allows us to practice writing and executing sql queries right from our browsers, whether we're in the lab or studying at home! it's been super useful for getting the hang of the syntax, and it makes learning database management so much more engaging and practical.

sql query practice 💻
kahoot champions! 🏆

to wrap up our database class for the week, our professor hosted a highly competitive kahoot quiz to test our knowledge! the energy in the room was electric as everyone fiercely competed for the top spots. incredibly, the top 3 spots on the podium were completely swept by nyp students! it was such a proud moment for all of us representing our home school here in korea. to make the competition even more exciting, our lecturer generously awarded bonus marks to everyone who placed in the top 10. it was a fantastic way to review the material and definitely kept us all on the edge of our seats!

along with our database practicals, we also have a major presentation coming up for our discrete mathematics class! we have to tackle complex logical proofs and mathematical concepts, breaking them down into a comprehensive presentation for the professor and our classmates. it's definitely a bit nerve-wracking to think about presenting in front of everyone, but putting the slides together is really helping us consolidate our understanding of the tricky theorems. we haven't presented yet—we're just preparing for it—but it will feel incredibly rewarding once we finally wrap it up and get it over with!

discrete maths! 📊

hi dojo & coco ichibanya

we also ordered the katsudon for 8,500 won, and once again, we were amazed by how affordable and generous the portions were! the breaded pork cutlet was fried to a pristine golden brown and gently simmered in a savory, slightly sweet dashi broth with perfectly cooked onions and egg. it was served steaming hot over rice, creating the ultimate comforting bowl. the egg was soft and custardy, binding everything together beautifully. finding such high-quality, soul-warming meals right inside our school feels like uncovering a well-kept secret. hi dojo has quickly become our favorite go-to spot between classes!

katsudon goodness 🍳
coco ichibanya! 🍛

speaking of katsu curry, we also had an incredible meal at coco ichibanya, a legendary spot that never disappoints! their japanese-style curry is world-famous, mostly because of the total customization—you can personalize everything from the rice portion to the exact spice level. we went all out and ordered their signature pork katsu curry. the pork cutlet was fried to absolute perfection, boasting a crispy panko crust that remarkably stayed crunchy even when drenched in their thick, savory curry sauce. pairing the tender meat with fluffy rice and piping hot curry created an explosion of comforting flavors. with wonderfully huge portions, coco ichibanya truly never misses when you need a hearty, soul-warming meal!

local samgyetang

as the weather got chilly, we warmed up with some samgyetang (ginseng chicken soup) at a cozy local store nearby. the restaurant's rustic interiors and the friendly ajumma made it feel just like a home-cooked meal! uniquely, the chicken wasn't stuffed with the usual glutinous rice; instead, we got a generous bowl of white rice on the side. the fall-off-the-bone tender meat and the deeply nourishing, ginseng-rich broth were absolutely perfect. dipping the meat in salt and pairing it with rice was the ultimate comfort food to end our busy week!

comforting soup 🍲

grocery run at emart

addicting! 🍌

later, we did our weekly grocery run at emart. wandering through the aisles of korean snacks and fresh food is a whole experience on its own. the one thing we absolutely can't get enough of is the classic korean banana milk! it is seriously so addicting and has become our daily staple. we recently learned that the iconic binggrae brand uses different cap colors to help you tell them apart—the classic original banana milk always has a green cap. if you see a light blue cap, that's the "light" version with lower calories and less sugar, and the black cap signifies their newer "zero" added sugar version! it's the perfect sweet and creamy pick-me-up after a long day of classes. picking up a fresh pack of these is always the highlight of our grocery trips!

besides stocking up on banana milk, we couldn't resist checking out emart's famous ready-to-eat deli section! they always have such an incredible variety of packaged meals, from fresh sushi to crispy fried chicken and full bento boxes. especially towards the end of the day, you can often find great discounts on these delicious meals. we picked up a few of these affordable goodies to bring back to the dorm for a late-night feast. the quality and convenience of grocery store food here in korea never ceases to amaze us!

emart goodies 🍱

isaac toast

scrambled ham cheese! 🥪

for a quick and delicious bite, we finally tried the famous isaac toast! there's a really convenient branch right along the stretch of shops just outside our school, super close to the subway exit. i ordered their classic scrambled ham cheese toast, and honestly, it completely exceeded my expectations. everything is made to order, so it was served piping hot with the sweet butter sizzling on the toast. the portion was surprisingly huge, especially considering it was only 4,200 won! the signature sweet and savory kiwi sauce combined with the fluffy scrambled eggs and melted cheese makes it absolutely addictive. it's truly the perfect, affordable on-the-go meal before heading to class!

our productive internship grind

even though we are having the absolute time of our lives exploring korea, reality still calls! while balancing our studies and traveling, we are also actively applying for internships back home. it definitely takes some extra effort and discipline to juggle networking, polishing our resumes, and keeping track of application deadlines while being on exchange. but setting aside some dedicated cafe study time to grind out these applications together makes the process a lot more manageable. keeping our fingers crossed for good news soon!

the grind never stops! 💼

from delicious campus eats to the productive internship grind—what a fulfilling week! 💼🥪🤍
