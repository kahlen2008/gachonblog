utopia concert 🎶

the absolute highlight of this week was gachon university's very own utopia festival—a massive 3-day concert held right on gachon's sports field! the entire campus was buzzing with excitement all week long as students prepared for what is arguably the biggest annual event on campus. the sports field was completely transformed into a full-blown concert venue, with a huge stage, dazzling lights, booming speakers, and a sea of students all gathered together under the night sky. the energy was absolutely electric from day 1, with incredible performances from talented artists and student acts that had the entire crowd singing, dancing, and waving their phone flashlights in unison. it was such a surreal experience to be part of a massive korean university festival—nothing quite like it back home!

utopia festival! 🎤✨
zico on stage! 🔥

but the real showstopper was day 3—the grand finale. the crowd absolutely erupted when zico took the stage! hearing "any song" and "new thing" performed live, right here on our own campus, was genuinely unreal. his stage presence was incredible, and the entire sports field turned into one giant party. the energy was on another level when meovv also performed, bringing their fierce choreography and powerful vocals that had everyone screaming. seeing these huge k-pop artists perform live at our own university festival was honestly a once-in-a-lifetime experience. we were packed shoulder to shoulder with thousands of students, all jumping and singing along at the top of our lungs. it's moments like these that make this exchange truly unforgettable!

beyond the incredible performances, the festival grounds were also lined with food stalls and pop-up booths that added to the whole vibrant atmosphere. students were grabbing tteokbokki, churros, steak, and all sorts of korean street food snacks between sets. there's something so special about eating freshly grilled food under the night sky while live music plays in the background. it honestly felt like a mini night market right on campus! the combination of amazing live music, delicious food, and the buzzing energy of thousands of excited students made utopia an experience we'll never forget.

festival food! 🍢

gachon maker space

this week, we discovered an absolute hidden gem on campus: the gachon maker space (also known as gachon maker city or gmct)! located all the way down in basement 3 of vision tower, it is essentially a creative platform to support students in realizing their innovative ideas. just like nyp, gachon has its very own makerspace packed with an incredible range of digital manufacturing tools, including 3d printers, laser cutters, uv printers, woodworking equipment, and even bio-3d printers!

maker space! 🛠️
so cool! ✨

we didn't actually go inside or make anything ourselves since you usually need to undergo basic training sessions before reserving the equipment. instead, we just admired the amazing facility from the outside and took some pictures of the incredible prototypes and products other students had created that were on display. seeing all these cool creations was super inspiring, even if we know we'll probably never actually go in!

classes & kahoot

classes are getting more intense, but our professors are finding fun ways to keep us engaged. in our database module this week, the professor hosted a massive kahoot session to review what we've learned so far! the best part? if you're in the top 5, you get bonus marks for the module! it got so competitive, and amazingly, the top 5 spots were entirely dominated by nyp students! it was such a hilarious and proud moment for all of us.

kahoot time! 🎮
virtual box setup 💻

speaking of classes getting intense, our operating systems module is in full swing! we had to set up a ubuntu virtual machine using virtualbox to start actively learning how to use the ubuntu operating system, explore linux commands, and understand system architecture. it was pretty challenging trying to configure all the settings correctly on our laptops without running into errors, but once everything finally booted up, it felt incredibly rewarding to have our very own virtual linux environment running smoothly!

seongsu-dong adventure

anmok gukbap 🍲

we decided to revisit seongsu-dong this weekend! the last time we were here, it suddenly started pouring rain, so we weren't able to stay out for very long and didn't get to fully explore the "brooklyn of seoul". this time, with the weather on our side, our first stop was anmok, a wildly popular michelin-recognized restaurant specializing in refined busan-style pork and rice soup (dwaeji gukbap). it's located about a 6-minute walk from exit 3 of seongsu station. we had to wait for an hour since there were 60 groups ahead of us, but it was absolutely worth it! their signature gukbap features a rich, 24-hour simmered broth made with domestic pork that is deeply flavorful and incredibly clean. served with unlimited fresh kimchi, kkakdugi, and cucumber peppers, it was the perfect comforting meal to fuel our shopping spree!

seongsu is undeniably a shopping paradise, filled with flagship stores and unique boutiques. we visited the blue elephant store, a wildly popular korean eyewear brand, to try on some stylish sunglasses. the interior of the store was stunningly aesthetic, feeling more like a modern art exhibition than a shop. they have so many trendy and vintage-inspired designs on display. we spent quite a while taking aesthetic mirror selfies and hunting for the perfect frames to match our outfits. the quality and designs were just incredible for the price point!

blue elephant 🕶️
olive young haul!

of course, absolutely no trip to seongsu is complete without a stop at olive young. this specific seongsu branch was absolutely massive and spanning multiple floors! it was packed with people hunting for the latest k-beauty trends. we spent well over an hour just wandering the aisles, swatching different lip tints on our hands until we ran out of space, trying out new viral korean makeup products we saw on tiktok, and stocking up on all our favorite skincare essentials like toner pads and pimple patches. walking out with a heavy, full bag of goodies felt incredibly satisfying!

to top off our seongsu adventure, we visited hanjungsun (한정선), a wildly popular premium fruit chapssaltteok (mochi) boutique. it's famous for its pillowy-soft dough and vibrant, juicy fillings wrapped in elegant traditional packaging. the queue was insane—there were 500 groups in front of us and we had to wait for 2 full hours! but getting our hands on the blueberry yoghurt mochi, muscat yoghurt mochi, and the red bean and butter mochi made it all worth it.

fruit mochi 🍓
miffy coasters! 🐰

while exploring the vibrant neighbourhood, we also unexpectedly stumbled upon a massive miffy pop-up store! the entire storefront was decorated so adorably, and the store inside was absolutely packed with the cutest miffy merchandise you could ever imagine—from plushies and keychains to aesthetic homeware and stationery. we spent so much time just fawning over how cute everything was. we naturally couldn't resist picking up some adorable souvenirs to bring a little bit of that cute seongsu magic back to decorate our dorm desks!

what we learned

  • ✔ gachon's utopia festival is an absolutely unmissable experience
  • ✔ seeing zico and meovv live on campus is a core memory forever
  • ✔ the maker space is the coolest hidden gem on campus
  • ✔ seongsu-dong's michelin food is totally worth the crazy wait times

from utopia festival to seongsu adventures—this was easily one of the best weeks yet! 🎶✈️🤍
