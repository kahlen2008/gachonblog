lotte mall

this week we decided to explore jamsil, specifically the overwhelmingly huge and glamorous lotte mall! walking through the seemingly endless floors of designer brands, pop-up stores, and bustling crowds was an experience in itself. however, our primary mission was to grab some amazing food, so we made a beeline straight to din tai fung. despite the wait, it was entirely worth it. we ordered their famous braised beef noodle soup and, of course, a steamer basket full of their signature xiao long bao (xlb). the soup was incredibly rich, with tender chunks of beef that melted in your mouth, perfectly chewy noodles, and a deeply aromatic, slightly spicy broth that warmed us up from the inside out.

beef noodle soup 🍜
xiao long bao! 🥟

the xiao long bao were just as spectacular as we remembered. carefully picking up the delicate dumpling, dipping it in the sharp black vinegar and ginger strips, and biting a small hole to let the savory, steaming soup pour out into the spoon is just the absolute best feeling. the burst of umami flavors was phenomenal. after satisfying our cravings, we couldn't leave jamsil without stopping by the infamous london bagel museum! the hype around this place is crazy—there is always a massive queue wrapping around the corner, and honestly, it completely lives up to it. the rustic, cozy interior mixed with the smell of freshly baked bread is heavenly. it literally feels like stepping into a vintage bakery in london. the display of bagels is so aesthetically pleasing that we spent a solid ten minutes just deciding what to get.

i specifically got the salt butter pretzel bagel, and it was a life-changing choice. it has the perfect shiny, slightly salty pretzel crust on the outside, but the inside is incredibly soft and chewy like a classic bagel. the creamy butter stuffed in the middle just melts in your mouth and perfectly balances out the savory salt crystals on top. if you're ever near jamsil, you absolutely have to brave the line and try it out. getting these amazing pastries totally made our day and fueled us up for more walking around the massive lotte mall.

salt butter pretzel 🥨

sweet treats

sweet ice box cake 🍰

back around school, i finally revisited the popular cafe chain i featured in my vlog—a twosome place! this cafe is absolutely everywhere in korea and is famous for its incredible dessert selection. i treated myself to their signature ice box cake, which is basically an artisanal korean take on cookies and cream. it features alternating layers of rich mascarpone cheese cream and dark chocolate oreo cookies that sit in the fridge until they meld together into a perfectly soft, cake-like texture that literally melts in your mouth. it's the perfect mid-week treat and gives such a massive sugar rush to keep the energy up during our long study sessions between classes!

hangul day

speaking of classes, friday was an absolute blessing because all classes were cancelled in observance of hangul day! hangul day celebrates the historical invention and proclamation of the korean alphabet by king sejong the great. the whole city felt vibrant and alive with the holiday spirit, and we were so grateful for the unexpected long weekend. we took the time to completely relax and celebrate the korean way by heading to a mall nearby for an epic, all-out samgyeopsal feast. there's nothing better than the sound of thick cuts of korean pork belly sizzling on a hot grill alongside garlic and kimchi. wrapping the perfectly crisped meat in fresh perilla leaves with a dab of savory ssamjang and sharing laughs with good friends is the absolute best way to celebrate a day off!

k-bbq feast! 🥩
thank you hangul day 🙏

having this extra pocket of free time in our busy schedule allowed us to catch up on some much-needed rest. we finally got to sleep in and later spent hours leisurely strolling and exploring more of the hidden local neighborhood spots that we usually just rush past on our hectic weekday commutes to the university. the beautiful autumn weather made the entire day feel magical, and we definitely made the absolute most out of our holiday break.

academics & midterms

as much as we've been loving the tourist life, reality is finally starting to set in—midterms are just around the corner! the academic pressure is slowly creeping up on us, and we are realizing that we seriously need to shift gears and start studying. our weekends of purely exploring are now being mixed with intense library sessions and cafe study dates. the sheer volume of lecture slides and materials we need to catch up on is a bit daunting, but brewing some strong coffee and tackling the notes together definitely makes the study grind a lot more bearable.

midterms looming 📚
learning new ERDs 📊

one of the biggest academic hurdles this week has been our database class, specifically learning how to draw entity-relationship (er) diagrams. surprisingly, gachon university's methodology and specific notation style for erds are quite different from what we originally learned back at nyp! at nyp, we were so used to using the specific oracle format, but here, the shapes, the way cardinality is indicated, and the overall structural rules have their own unique flair. it's taking some serious mental gymnastics to unlearn our old habits and adapt to this new standard, but figuring out the logic together has actually been a really fun and rewarding challenge!

another major subject keeping us busy is our operating systems class. this week, we've been diving deep into process creation and termination. it's a surprisingly intricate topic—learning exactly how the os manages memory, forks new child processes, and safely handles their execution and eventual termination has been fascinating but definitely brain-draining. the stakes feel even higher right now because our professor just announced that this is the absolute final topic being tested for our upcoming os midterms! it means we really have to lock in, review all the technical details, and make sure we perfectly understand the lifecycle of a process before exam day.

os process lifecycle 💻

internships

amidst all the fun and eating, reality still calls, and we also spent a considerable amount of time this week reflecting on our professional goals and checking in on our internship application progress back home. the hustle really paid off, and it is incredibly exciting because some of us actually received official interview offers! juggling the stress of applications, tailoring resumes, and scheduling interviews across completely different time zones while trying to fully immerse ourselves in the exchange experience is undeniably tough. it takes a lot of discipline, but seeing the hard work finally yield results brings such an immense wave of relief and validation. we are all enthusiastically cheering each other on as we spend hours at local cafes doing intense prep for these upcoming technical and behavioral calls.

interview prep! 💻

suseo station

massive suseo stn 🚆

later in the week, we took a purposeful trip down to suseo station just to scope it out. for those who haven't been, it's a massive, ultra-modern transit hub right in the heart of the bustling gangnam district. it serves as the northern starting point for the srt (super rapid train), which is a sleek high-speed rail network that shoots straight down the peninsula to southern provinces like busan and mokpo at unbelievable speeds! the station architecture is stunning with its towering glass facades, and it's totally integrated with the deep underground subway lines, making it exceptionally convenient to access from anywhere in seoul. experiencing the sheer scale of the station and watching the sleek trains pull in was super impressive, and it has definitely inspired us to seriously start planning a weekend getaway down south very soon!

what we learned

  • ✔ london bagel museum is 100% worth the hype
  • ✔ hangul day is the best holiday ever (thank you for the long weekend!)
  • ✔ gachon's ERD notation is totally different from nyp's, but we're adapting
  • ✔ suseo station's srt is the ultimate cheat code for traveling south
  • ✔ balancing fun and internship interviews is tough but rewarding

from dumplings to high speed trains—another amazing week down! 🚆🥯
