dorm life & eats

dorm beef bowl (900₩ rice!) 🍳

and of course, dorm cooking continues to be our favourite wallet-friendly routine to recharge between study sessions! this week we whipped up a colourful, balanced beef and egg rice bowl made with tender thinly sliced beef, poached eggs with perfectly runny yolks, and crisp romaine lettuce, all sourced during our grocery run at e-mart. the absolute undisputed holy grail of budget student living in korea is e-mart's no brand (노브랜드) aisle! while branded supermarket items in korea can quickly drive up your monthly grocery bill, no brand offers daily kitchen staples at unbeatable discounts. case in point: their bowls of microwaveable instant white rice (햇반) cost only around 900 won each — which translates to less than $1 SGD! setting the beef atop a bed of shredded fresh romaine, and topping it with a runny egg over piping-hot 900-won rice created a satisfying and nutritious meal right in our dorm pantry for just a couple of dollars!

what we learnt

  • ✔ e-mart no brand 900-won rice (< $1 SGD) keeps dorm home cooking affordable
