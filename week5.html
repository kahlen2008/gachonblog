shinsegae

we did some exploring around the massive shinsegae department store. it's truly a maze of luxury brands and trendy pop-ups, but the real star of the show is the food hall in the basement. it is absolutely legendary! there are endless aisles of beautifully packaged sweets, artisanal bakeries, premium meats, and a dizzying array of international cuisines. you could easily spend hours just wandering around the stalls and marvel at all the high-end delicacies. the atmosphere is bustling and so vibrant—it's sensory overload in the best way possible.

shinsegae market 🏢
one degree north 🍽️

out of all the incredible options, we decided to eat at one degree north, which actually boasts a michelin star! it's famous for serving authentic, high-quality singaporean-style roasted meats. the picture shows their signature bowl, which came with perfectly roasted duck and roasted pork that literally melted in our mouths. the skin was so crispy, the meat was incredibly juicy and tender, and the rich sauce tied everything together flawlessly. having a taste of home that is elevated to a michelin-star level right here in seoul was an unforgettable culinary experience!

while wandering around the house of shinsegae, we also stumbled upon "twelve," a premium wellness-focused grocery and food hall concept that people have dubbed the "korean erewhon." they have a signature "twelve wonder bar" that serves freshly blended wellness smoothies. we decided to try their "green echo" smoothie, which contained a healthy mix of spinach, basil, greek yoghurt, pineapple, stevia, and more. it came with a staggering price tag, though! while it was definitely nice, very healthy, and refreshing, it was just way too expensive for what it is. it's a cool luxury wellness experience to try once, but we're definitely never buying it again!

way too expensive! 💸

a hidden gem: hi dojo

butadon for 6900₩! 🥩

back on campus, we discovered an absolute hidden gem called hi dojo! tucked away in a quiet corner near the school's mrt entrance at basement 3 of vision tower, this cozy little eatery is a life-saver for hungry students on a budget. the moment you step in, you are greeted by the incredibly nice staff and the comforting aroma of sizzling meat. the store actually has two levels, with level 2 offering plenty more seats if the ground floor is full! their menu is incredibly affordable without compromising on quality or size. i tried their signature butadon for just 6,900 won, and it was a masterpiece—tender, deeply marinated slices of pork perfectly caramelized and layered generously over a hot bed of fluffy rice. the sweet and savory sauce coated every grain, making each bite an absolute delight!

we also ordered the katsu curry for 8,900 won, and we were completely blown away by the generous portions! the pork cutlet was fried to a pristine golden brown, boasting a satisfying crunch that held up beautifully against the rich, thick, and deeply flavorful japanese-style curry. finding such high-quality, comforting meals right inside our school at these prices feels like uncovering a well-kept secret. hi dojo has quickly become our favorite go-to spot between classes, proving that you don't need to venture far or spend a lot to enjoy an incredibly satisfying, soul-warming meal on campus.

katsu curry crunch 🍛

local samgyetang

comforting soup 🍲

as the weather got chilly, we warmed up with some samgyetang (ginseng chicken soup) at a cozy local store nearby. the restaurant's rustic interiors and the friendly ajumma made it feel just like a home-cooked meal! uniquely, the chicken wasn't stuffed with the usual glutinous rice; instead, we got a generous bowl of white rice on the side. the fall-off-the-bone tender meat and the deeply nourishing, ginseng-rich broth were absolutely perfect. dipping the meat in salt and pairing it with rice was the ultimate comfort food to end our busy week!

database group assignment

it wasn't all just eating and exploring this week—we also had to focus on academics! in our database class, the professor just briefed us about our very first major group assignment, which will involve designing a relational database schema from scratch. we haven't actually started on the heavy lifting yet, but the most exciting part was the group formation process! we got to mingle with other international students and ended up forming our groups and making new friends with classmates from countries like lithuania and vietnam. it's so cool to experience such a diverse, multicultural classroom environment, and we're really looking forward to collaborating on the project together!

brainstorming! 💻

semester 1 results

hard work pays off! 💯

on top of all the new classes here, this week also brought some huge news from back home—our nyp semester 1 gpa results were officially released! it was incredibly nerve-wracking to check our grades while being thousands of miles away in another country. waiting for the portal to load felt like the longest few minutes ever! but honestly, the overwhelming wave of relief and pride that washed over us made it all worth it. seeing all the late nights, endless assignments, and sheer hard work from last semester finally pay off and come to life in those numbers was the best feeling. it gave us a massive motivation boost to keep working just as hard for our current semester here in korea!

chuseok break!

the best surprise of the week came from our professors—some of them actually cancelled classes because of the upcoming chuseok (korean thanksgiving) holiday! chuseok is one of the biggest holidays in korea, and the festive mood is already in the air. getting that unexpected time off was a huge blessing, giving us some much-needed time to rest, recharge, and catch up on our sleep after all the crazy exploring and assignments. it was the perfect breather we didn't know we needed. we spent the extra free time just chilling at home, unwinding, and mentally preparing ourselves for the rest of the semester. honestly, random cancelled classes are the absolute best!

unexpected day off! 💤

what we learned

  • ✔ shinsegae's food hall is an absolute paradise
  • ✔ one degree north's michelin-star roasted meats are a taste of home
  • ✔ twelve at shinsegae really is the korean erewhon (and just as expensive)
  • ✔ hi dojo in vision tower is a top-tier hidden gem for campus eats
  • ✔ local samgyetang spots have the best cozy atmospheres
  • ✔ making new international friends in class is the best part of group assignments!
  • ✔ seeing your hard work pay off in your gpa is the ultimate motivation booster!
  • ✔ unexpected cancelled classes before holidays are the biggest blessing!

from shinsegae to delicious campus eats—another week of discoveries! 🛒✈️🤍
