campus transit

usually, our campus shuttle service (the famous "ladybug" shuttle) winds down around 5:30pm. but on certain busy evenings when students are staying late on campus, the shuttle service unexpectedly extends until 6:00pm or even 6:30pm! even better, instead of those smaller red shuttle buses we showed in previous weeks, the university rolls out a massive white charter bus that can seat about double the capacity. after spending long hours in classrooms and libraries, having a warm, spacious bus waiting right at the stop to whisk us up the steep hill to our dorms was an absolute blessing!

evening shuttle savior! 🚌

comfort eats

for lunch, we headed straight back to our trusty favorite spot near campus — hi dojo! i had previously ordered their regular katsudon and loved it, but this time i decided to upgrade to their cheese katsudon (9,000 won). it was absolutely phenomenal — the freshly fried pork cutlet was thick, juicy, and covered in a generous, bubbling blanket of melted mozzarella cheese that gave an incredible cheese pull with every bite. also, in case we haven't mentioned it before, hi dojo has a great self-service station where you can help yourself to unlimited free sides, including delicious chilled kimchi and pickled radishes! having that sharp, spicy kimchi on hand to cut through the rich, cheesy tonkatsu makes the whole meal perfectly balanced.

cheese katsudon (9,000₩) 🧀

what we learned

  • ✔ campus shuttle sometimes runs until 6:30pm with a double-capacity white bus
  • ✔ hi dojo's cheese katsudon (9,000 won) with free kimchi is top tier comfort food
