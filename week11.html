comfort eats

cheese katsudon (9,000₩) 🧀

back around campus, we headed straight to our trusty favourite spot near gachon—hi dojo! i had previously fallen in love with their classic katsudon, but this time i upgraded to their cheese katsudon (9,000 won). it was utterly phenomenal: a thick, juicy, freshly fried pork cutlet blanketed under a generous, molten sheet of melted mozzarella cheese that produced an impressive cheese pull with every bite. plus, hi dojo offers unlimited free sides at their self-service bar—their crisp, spicy chilled kimchi is the perfect tangy counterbalance to cut through the rich, cheesy pork!

between busy study sessions, cooking meals in our dorm kitchen remains one of the best ways to stay healthy on a student budget. this week i whipped up a hearty beef bowl using fresh romaine lettuce, sunny eggs, and thinly sliced beef from e-mart. here's a golden tip for anyone studying abroad in korea: always check the e-mart no brand (노브랜드) section! branded groceries in korea can be pricey, but no brand products offer identical quality at unbeatable discounts. for example, a bowl of no brand microwaveable instant rice costs only around 900 won—less than $1 SGD! pan-frying seasoned beef, pairing it with crisp greens, a runny fried egg, and hot 900-won rice made for a restaurant-worthy dorm feast for just a few dollars.

dorm beef bowl (900₩ rice!) 🍳

what we learnt

  • ✔ hi dojo's cheese katsudon (9,000 won) with free kimchi is top tier comfort food
  • ✔ e-mart no brand rice is only ~900 won (< $1 SGD), ideal for dorm meals
